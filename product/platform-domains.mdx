Every BMDRM resource is assigned a default platform domain so it works immediately. You can also add custom hostnames to serve traffic on your own domains with managed certificates.

## Default platform domains

When you create a resource, BMDRM provisions it under a default domain such as:

```text
*.b-cdn.bmdrm.net
```

Replace the wildcard with your resource identifier to access content directly. These domains are valid, pre-configured, and ready for immediate testing.

## Custom hostnames overview

Adding a custom hostname lets you serve traffic on your own domain. BMDRM handles SSL/TLS provisioning, renewal, and revocation automatically once DNS verification succeeds.

<Warning>
  Do not point production traffic to a custom hostname until DNS verification and certificate issuance are complete.
</Warning>

## Add a custom domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Domains settings">
    In the dashboard, go to **Domains \> Custom Hostnames** and click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your domain">
    Provide the fully qualified domain name you want to add (for example, `cdn.example.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify DNS ownership">
    BMDRM provides a TXT record. Add it to your DNS provider and click **Verify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for certificate issuance">
    After verification, BMDRM requests and installs a managed SSL/TLS certificate. This usually completes within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update DNS to BMDRM">
    Point your domain to the provided BMDRM target using a CNAME record. Traffic will route through the platform once DNS propagates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## SSL/TLS certificates

Managed certificates are automatically renewed. If you require a specific certificate configuration, contact support.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Auto-managed">
    BMDRM provisions and renews certificates without any action required.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bring your own">
    Enterprise plans can upload custom certificates through the API or dashboard.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Certificate issuance may be delayed if DNS records are cached or if the domain has CAA restrictions.
</Note>
