BMDRM gives you full control over your account from one central settings page. You can update your personal profile, secure your account with two-factor authentication (2FA), generate API keys for automation, and choose which notifications you receive.

This page covers common account management tasks and where to find each setting in the dashboard.

## Update Your Profile Info

Keeping your profile up to date ensures BMDRM can reach you with important account alerts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Sign in to the BMDRM dashboard, click your avatar in the top-right corner, and select **Account Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Your Details">
    Update your display name, email address, company name, and time zone. Click **Save Changes** when finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Changing your email address will trigger a verification email. You must confirm the new address before it takes effect.
</Note>

## Change Your Password

For security, BMDRM recommends rotating your password every 90 days.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Security">
    In **Account Settings**, select the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Password">
    Enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password. Click **Update Password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Choose a strong password with at least 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
</Warning>

## Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Adding 2FA protects your account even if your password is compromised.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Setup">
    In **Account Settings \> Security**, click **Enable 2FA**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR Code">
    Use an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy) to scan the QR code displayed on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and Save">
    Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app to confirm, then store your backup codes in a safe place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create an API Key

API keys let scripts and applications interact with BMDRM on your behalf.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open API Keys">
    Navigate to **Account Settings \> API Keys**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a New Key">
    Click **Create API Key**, give it a descriptive name (e.g., "CI/CD Automation"), and select the scopes you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and Store">
    Copy the key immediately. BMDRM shows it only once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create separate API keys for different services so you can revoke one without affecting others.
</Tip>

## Manage Notification Preferences

Control which events send you email or push alerts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    In **Account Settings**, click the **Notifications** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Channels">
    Enable or disable alerts for billing events, security warnings, product updates, and team activity by email or dashboard notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Preferences">
    Click **Save Preferences** to apply your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
