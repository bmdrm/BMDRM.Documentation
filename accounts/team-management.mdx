BMDRM lets you share account access with your team while keeping control over who can manage resources, view billing, or change settings. Each member is assigned a role that defines what they can see and do.

This page explains how to invite members, how each role works, and how to remove someone from your team.

## Invite a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    In the BMDRM dashboard, click your avatar, then choose **Team Management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an Invite">
    Click **Invite Member**, enter the person's email address, select a role (Owner, Admin, Member, or Read-only), and click **Send Invitation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept Invitation">
    The invitee will receive an email with a secure link to join your team. Invitations expire after 7 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can resend or cancel a pending invitation from the same page.
</Tip>

## Roles and Permissions

BMDRM uses four roles. Permissions are cumulative; higher roles include the abilities of lower roles.

| Permission | Owner | Admin | Member | Read-only |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| View all projects | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| View billing and invoices | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Create and edit resources | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Invite and manage members | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Change billing and payment info | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Delete projects or resources | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Manage API keys and integrations | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Change or cancel the account | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |

<Note>
  Only the Owner can delete the account or transfer ownership. Each team must have exactly one Owner.
</Note>

## Change a Member's Role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Member">
    Go to **Team Management** and locate the person in the member list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Role">
    Click the role dropdown next to their name, select the new role, and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Actions">
    In **Team Management**, find the member and click the **More** menu (three dots).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove">
    Select **Remove from Team** and confirm. The person loses access immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a member does not delete resources they created. Transfer ownership of critical resources before removing an Owner.
</Warning>
