BMDRM uses usage-based billing, so you pay for the resources you consume each month. The billing dashboard lets you add payment methods, download past invoices, set spending limits, and enable auto-recharge to avoid service interruptions.

This page covers how to manage your billing settings and understand your monthly charges.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find my billing dashboard?">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner of the BMDRM dashboard, then select **Billing** from the menu.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Add or Update a Payment Method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Methods">
    In the BMDRM billing dashboard, go to the **Payment Methods** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Card">
    Click **Add Payment Method**, enter your card or bank details, and click **Save**. The name and billing address must match your payment provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can store multiple payment methods and mark one as the default for automatic charges.
</Tip>

## View and Download Invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invoices">
    In the billing dashboard, select the **Invoices** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Click any invoice row to view a summary, then click **Download PDF** for your records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invoices are generated after each billing cycle and can be downloaded for up to 24 months.
</Note>

## Usage-Based Billing

BMDRM measures resource consumption each hour and rolls it into a single monthly invoice. Charges vary based on bandwidth, requests, storage, compute time, and other metrics.

## Enable Auto-Recharge

Auto-recharge automatically tops up your balance when it falls below a threshold you set, helping prevent service interruptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Auto-Recharge">
    In the billing dashboard, click the **Auto-Recharge** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Choose a trigger balance and a recharge amount, select a payment method, and toggle **Enable Auto-Recharge**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Auto-recharge uses your default payment method. Keep a valid card on file to avoid failed charges.
</Warning>

## Set Spending Limits

You can configure a monthly spending limit to receive alerts or pause non-essential services when costs approach a budget.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Spending Limits">
    In the billing dashboard, go to the **Spending Limits** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Limit">
    Enter a monthly cap amount, choose whether to receive an email alert or pause services, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Spending limits are advisory or hard stops depending on your selection. Critical services are not paused automatically.
</Note>
