The BMDRM Affiliate Program rewards you for introducing new users to the platform. Whether you run a blog, a YouTube channel, or a developer community, you can earn a commission for every qualifying customer who signs up through your referral link.

This page explains how to join, how commissions work, and how to get paid.

## How to Join

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Visit the BMDRM Affiliate Program page and click **Apply Now**. Fill out the short application with your contact details and promotion channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Approval">
    Applications are reviewed within 2 business days. You will receive an email once your account is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Your Link">
    After approval, log in to the affiliate dashboard to find your unique referral link and marketing materials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Referral Link

Your unique referral link tracks every visitor and conversion back to your account. Place it in your content, email newsletters, or social media posts.

- The link uses a cookie to track referrals for up to 90 days
- You receive credit when a new user signs up and meets the qualifying criteria
- You can generate custom links with UTM parameters for different campaigns

<Note>
  Referrals must be new BMDRM accounts. Self-referrals, existing accounts, and accounts created through other channels are not eligible for commission.
</Note>

## Commission Structure

BMDRM offers a tiered commission rate based on your performance. Commission details are shown in your affiliate dashboard when you log in. As a guideline, affiliates can earn up to X% of the referred user's net qualified spend.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When are commissions approved?">
    Commissions enter a pending status for a short review period to account for refunds and chargebacks. After the review period, they are approved and added to your balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a minimum payout threshold?">
    Yes. You must reach a minimum balance before a payout is issued. The exact threshold is visible in your affiliate dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payout Methods

BMDRM supports multiple payout options for your convenience. You can choose your preferred method in the affiliate dashboard under **Payout Settings**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bank Transfer" icon="building-columns">
    Receive payouts directly to your bank account via wire or ACH transfer.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Digital Wallets" icon="wallet">
    Choose from supported digital wallet providers for faster, lower-fee transfers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Update your payout details and tax forms in the dashboard before your first withdrawal to avoid delays.
</Tip>

## Program Terms

By participating in the program, you agree to BMDRM's affiliate terms and policies. Commissions are subject to review and may be adjusted for fraudulent or prohibited activity.

For full terms and conditions, refer to the [Affiliate Terms link in your dashboard](https://dashboard.bmdrm.com/affiliates/terms).

<Warning>
  Do not use your referral link on coupon sites, in paid search ads on BMDRM branded keywords, or in any misleading context. Violations may result in removal from the program and forfeiture of commissions.
</Warning>
