You can customize the appearance of the bunny.net dashboard by choosing between light and dark mode.

## Change your theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Appearance Icon">
    Locate the appearance icon in top-left corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your theme">
    Click the light, dark, or system mode icon to switch themes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
